# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, no dependencies. Deployable to any static host.

## Users

Homeowners, owners of plots (parcelas, fincas, solares), community administrators, businesses and public bodies in Ávila and surrounding area who have a tree that is too tall, dangerous, dead, touching a roof or power line, or simply needs pruning, and who cannot or should not do it themselves. Usually they arrive from a phone, often after a storm or a warning from a neighbour, and want to know quickly whether El Moreno can do it and how to ask for a quote.

## Product Purpose

One-page website for El Moreno, a tree felling and high-pruning service (trabajos verticales / arborist climbing). Success = a visitor contacts by WhatsApp, phone or the quote form.

## Positioning

Works at height with rope access and professional equipment, so it reaches trees where machinery cannot go (patios, gardens next to houses), and leaves the site clean: removal of debris is included.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Services (from the client's flyer):
- Talas de árboles: segura y controlada, incluye retirada de residuos.
- Podas en altura: para árboles sanos, seguros y con mejor crecimiento.
- Retirada de restos: de la poda o tala, dejando la zona limpia y ordenada.
- Mantenimiento de zonas verdes: hogares, parcelas, empresas y espacios públicos.

Claims from the flyer: personal capacitado, equipos de seguridad, experiencia y responsabilidad, presupuestos sin coste.

No backend: the demo form validates and opens WhatsApp/email or states clearly it does not send to a server.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: «El Moreno» — tagline «Talas y podas en altura».
- Slogans: «Tu espacio seguro, nuestro compromiso», «Árboles cuidados, espacios más seguros».
- Visual identity binding: the client's flyer — dark forest green, lime green, yellow highlight brush strokes, heavy condensed uppercase type, arborist climbing photography.
- Language: Spanish from Spain (tuteo), not voseo.

1. Contact in one tap from any screen.
2. Safety first: say how the work is done, not just that it is safe.

4. Local and direct: a person with a name, not a faceless company.
